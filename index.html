About

I like work that sits between disciplines.

My work spans global B2B marketing, digital products, brand and growth, and leadership education. I’m especially interested in turning complex strategy into something teams can actually execute.

Based in Tokyo, I spend a lot of time thinking about how Japanese companies can communicate, compete, and lead more effectively around the world.
